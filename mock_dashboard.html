Proposed Solution

The DigitalTwin.ai Engine

1. Modeling the Path

Mapping the physical line to a digital graph model.

  • Nodes & Edges: Stations act as nodes, conveyors as edges.
  • Targeted Focus: Tracking critical junctures to reduce compute overhead.

2. Capturing Data

Bridging physical actions and digital data.

  • Active IoT: Smart tools push real-time data via MQTT.
  • Proxy Metrics: Tracking power draw or RFID dwell times.

3. Prediction

Shifting from visualization to prediction.

  • Bottlenecks: ARIMA and GNNs predict queue pile-ups.
  • Defects: XGBoost calculates live passing/failing probability.

4. Auto Rework

Autonomous execution to defeat the Rule of Ten.

  • Instant Reroute: Diverts high-risk parts to local rework.
  • Avoids the 10x penalty of final QA discovery.
